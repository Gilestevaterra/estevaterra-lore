## Description

The northwestern landmass of Estevaterra, shared by [[Political_Divisions/Crucaea|Crucaea]] and [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore|Laiqamore]] — one continuous coastline, no water gap between the two kingdoms. Divided internally by [[Geography/Bezmoria/Zorayavan_Spine]]. Bordered by the [[Geography/Tisver_Ocean/Tisver_Ocean|Tisver Ocean]], the [[Geography/Tisver_Ocean/Rigesonne_Depths|Rigesonne Depths]], the [[Geography/Sea_of_Leeronto/Sea_of_Leeronto|Sea of Leeronto]], [[Geography/Santerel_Deep/Santerel_Deep|Santerel Deep]], and the [[Geography/Wynne_Gulf/Wynne_Gulf|Wynne Gulf]].
