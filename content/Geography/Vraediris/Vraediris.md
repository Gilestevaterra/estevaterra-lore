## Description

A barren, uncharted island reached by rowboat from ship anchorage off its east coast — no docks, no known civilization. Believed by the party and [[Characters/NPCs/Benedict_Glorybluff|Commander Glorybluff]]'s expedition to be an uninhabited, deadly wasteland on arrival, though that assumption doesn't hold: the island turns out to be home to [[Factions/Leonin|the Leonin]], and also to a hired Laiqamorian mercenary company under [[Characters/NPCs/Norjor_Fogflow|Norjor Fogflow]], also searching for the Sphere. Home to [[Magic/Items/Luminous_Sphere_of_Eldrida|the Luminous Sphere of Eldrida]] and the guardians said to protect it, and to [[Qalb_Al-Rimal|Qalb Al-Rimal]], "Heart of the Sands," a further destination reached deeper inland via the mountains.

The interior is desert throughout, split into a few distinct zones visible from the landing beach: [[Oasis_Entrance|an oasis]] deep in the desert, marked by large cacti; towering dunes leading to [[Hills_Entrance|large hills]] southwest toward a mountain range; and [[Canyon_Entrance|a canyon]] cutting between the mountains, possibly an old riverbed. The beach itself and the open desert are just alternate travel methods for reaching these three, not destinations of their own — confirmed by Shane. The mountain/canyon route leads on to [[Qalb_Al-Rimal|Qalb Al-Rimal]], a ruined city built into the mountain — believed to be where the Luminous Sphere is kept. A blue dragon guardian was originally mentioned only in Shane's own planning notes and closed as a personal note-to-self, not committed content, but [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]]'s own planning notes reintroduce blue dragons as a genuinely intended inhabitant of the city's Palace district — confirmed by Shane as expected reuse of his own earlier planning notes, not a contradiction, see that Act's Designer Notes.

The desert itself is a genuine hazard: extreme heat, punishing thirst, mirages, sudden sandstorms, and predators like the giant scorpions that nest beneath the sand. Scattered debris throughout the dunes (a busted sand skiff, a tattered shield, an old boot, a red-ant-covered skeleton, giant ~10-foot rib bones) hints that people — and perhaps other creatures — have passed through before, despite the island's "uninhabited" reputation.

## Inhabitants & Wildlife

- [[Factions/Leonin]], the D\&D lion-folk species — confirmed by Shane to exist ONLY on this island in this world, nowhere else in Estevaterra. Native to [[Leonin_Cave_Settlement|a hidden cave settlement]]; capture and imprison outsiders on arrival, but extend real respect and alliance once tested, see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]].
- [[Factions/Eldrokai]], dimensional raiders who periodically invade via portal magic — the Leonin's ancient enemy, hold no territory of their own on the island.
- Giant Scorpions, nesting beneath the sand (see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_5]]'s Encounter FtK10).
- 3 Lamia, guarding [[Trial_Mountain]] and its water source with illusion magic (see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]]'s Encounter FtK24).
- A blue dragon (possibly with younger dragons as well) is planned as a guardian tied to Qalb Al-Rimal's Palace district and the Luminous Sphere decision, per [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]]'s planning notes — not yet actually encountered. Confirmed by Shane as expected reuse of his own earlier planning notes (originally closed as just a personal note-to-self), not a contradiction.
- [[Characters/NPCs/Norjor_Fogflow|Norjor Fogflow]]'s hired Laiqamorian mercenary company (1 Sorcerer, 4 Archers) — confirmed as of [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_7]], ambushes the party in a desert canyon en route to [[Qalb_Al-Rimal]], and Norjor himself (a Firbolg) reappears alone inside the city in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]].
- [[Bestiary/Hobgoblin_Captain|Hobgoblins]], [[Bestiary/Fire_Giant|Fire Giants]], [[Bestiary/Cyclops|Cyclopes]], and [[Bestiary/Desert_Howler|Desert Howlers]] — rival factions and fiends fighting over the ruins of Qalb Al-Rimal itself, see that page.

## History

Reached by ship from [[Geography/Alduria/Vauxier_Cove]], sailing out of [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Aprea/Bangola/Bangola]]. The destination of the party's quest to recover [[Magic/Items/Luminous_Sphere_of_Eldrida|the Luminous Sphere of Eldrida]] before [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore]]'s agents can — see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_2]] for the original offer. The voyage there was interrupted by [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_4|the murder of Captain Stormwind]]; landfall and the desert trek inland are detailed in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_5]]; the island's native Leonin, their Eldrokai invaders, and the mountain water trial are detailed in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]]; the journey on to Qalb Al-Rimal, including the first appearance of Laiqamore's own rival search party, is detailed in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_7]]; the city's own ruins begin being explored in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]]. Long ago, per Leonin oral history, the island was the site of "the great rebellion," when the Leonin drove off the Eldrokai after a period of enslavement — [[Qalb_Al-Rimal|the city]] believed to hold the Sphere dates from that era, and its own old gods were cast out by its people once they too learned to wield magic — the same underlying concept as the vault's real [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/|Old Gods]] and Divine Divide. **Confirmed by Shane:** the god tied to Qalb Al-Rimal is [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Avaretal|Avaretal]], Empress of Fire — the city fell within her canonical lands in Estevaterra, though the full extent of her domain elsewhere in the world is still to be mapped out once Shane divides the world among the pantheon; see [[Qalb_Al-Rimal]]'s own Designer Notes.
