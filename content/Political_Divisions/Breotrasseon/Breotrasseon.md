# 🗺️ Breotrasseon

## At a Glance

- **Level:** Kingdom
- **Parent Division:** _(top level, no parent)_
- **Government Type:** Monarchy, seated by the Rite of Recognition; currently held by Casa de Bragança
- **Ruler/Leader:** [[Characters/NPCs/Queen_Adelina]]
- **Dominant Faction:** [[Factions/Casa_de_Bragança]]

## Description

Occupies the eastern coast of the continent of [[Geography/Alduria/Alduria|Alduria]], fronting the Montaumart Ocean, with a scatter of small islands offshore to the north. Its only neighbor on the continent is [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Kingdom_of_Yeripia|Yeripia]], the two kingdoms divided by [[Geography/Alduria/Ribadouro_Peaks|the Ribadouro Peaks]], the mountainous stretch that runs along Breotrasseon's own northern reach. The kingdom divides into two Ducados: Ribamar, the southern peninsula and its one outlying island, and Valduero, the northern coastline the Peaks themselves belong to.

It is a small kingdom, smaller than Yeripia by a wide margin, and it carries itself that way. Ask someone's name here and more often than not you'll get their Casa's name in the same breath, since a family's line matters at least as much as the land it happens to hold. Breotrasseon is a coastal people through and through: fishing fleets and merchant convoys crowd every harbor, boatbuilding and navigation are trades taught almost as early as reading, and a household that hasn't lost someone to the sea, for better or worse, is the rare one rather than the common one.

The crown itself has answered to the Rite of Recognition for generations: no claimant may simply seize it, only be recognized for it, proven genuinely wanted by the kingdom's great houses and tested by old magic bound into the crown itself.

By long-standing convention, whichever Casa the Rite recognizes relocates entirely: the new monarch's family leaves its home Condado for [[Political_Divisions/Breotrasseon/Ducado_de_Ribamar/Condado_de_Figueira/Cambieira/Cambieira|Cambieira]], where it takes up residence at the [[Political_Divisions/Breotrasseon/Ducado_de_Ribamar/Condado_de_Figueira/Cambieira/Paço_Real|Paço Real]], and a new family is raised to rule the Condado they vacate. [[Factions/Casa_de_Bragança|Casa de Bragança]] itself rose to the throne this same way, generations back, and has held the Paço Real ever since.
